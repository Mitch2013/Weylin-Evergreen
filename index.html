Backstory

Weylin Evergreen grew up in the Twists of the Crescent, raised primarily by his mother while his father spent extended periods at sea working as a ship guard. His mother was a caring cleric who used her healing magic to help the poor. However, his father strongly disapproved of her magic because of society's distrust of it, and the two frequently argued. Despite his father's opinions, his mother taught Weylin the basics of her faith and healing abilities.

One day, while his father was away, his mother disappeared in the market district. She was later found dead, presumably killed by the Red Hand, though this was never proven. His father returned months later and believed her death was her own fault. With his mother gone, Weylin followed in his father's footsteps, training as a fighter and becoming a soldier. He now works as a known sword for hire.

His Beliefs

Although Weylin keeps the magic his mother taught him hidden from society, he has never abandoned the faith she instilled in him. Her memory reminds him that magic can be used to help those who cannot protect themselves. Beneath his armor and the reputation he has built as a fighter, Weylin still carries his mother's teachings and the belief that the helpless deserve protection.
